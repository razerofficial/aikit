# Code of Conduct – Razer AIKit

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of **Razer AIKit** pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free, inclusive, and responsible experience for everyone.  

Razer AIKit is an **R&D toolkit for Large Language Models (LLMs)** that plays a **critical role in enabling responsible AI development**.  

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to a safe, transparent, ethical, and collaborative ecosystem, supporting fairness, explainability, and robustness in AI research while adhering to comprehensive AI governance principles.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and respect toward other people  
- Supporting open, constructive, and inclusive collaboration  
- Taking responsibility for contributions, including safety and ethical implications  
- Sharing knowledge and encouraging responsible practices  
- Reporting vulnerabilities, risks, or potential harms responsibly  
- Implementing human-centered design principles that prioritize inclusivity and accessibility
- Conducting regular AI risk assessments and ethical impact evaluations
- Maintaining comprehensive documentation and audit trails for AI decisions
- Engaging in continuous learning about AI governance and regulatory compliance  

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind  
- Misuse of Razer AIKit to develop or deploy unsafe, biased, or harmful AI systems  
- Attempting to conceal known issues with fairness, robustness, or safety  
- Publishing others' private information without consent  
- Sharing malicious code or exploits without following [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) disclosure guidelines  
- Creating or distributing defamatory content without proper disclaimers or verification
- Circumventing security controls or access management systems
- Using AI systems to manipulate, exploit, or cause harm to individuals or groups
- Failing to implement required child safety protections or parental consent mechanisms
- Ignoring environmental impact considerations in AI development and deployment

---

## Responsible AI and Ethics

All contributors and users are expected to uphold our commitment to responsible AI across the following key areas:  

### Data Privacy and Protection
- **Data Collection:** Collect only necessary data with explicit user consent where required. Minimize data usage through anonymization and pseudonymization techniques.
- **Data Storage:** Store data securely with proper access controls. Prohibit unauthorized sharing or transfer of data without legal safeguards.
- **Usage Rights:** Implement processes for data access, correction, deletion, and objection requests (right to be forgotten).

### Child Safety and Parental Consent
- **Protection of Minors:** AI systems must not collect, process, or store child data without verifiable parental or guardian consent.
- **Age Verification:** Implement age verification protocols for AI systems that interact with users or collect personal data.
- **Restricted Use:** Child data must not be used for profiling, targeted advertising, or automated decision-making.

### Information Security
- **Platform Usage:** Use only approved enterprise-level AI platforms when uploading confidential and proprietary content. Do not upload confidential information into publicly available AI platforms.
- **Security Controls:** Implement robust cybersecurity measures, including encryption, firewalls, intrusion detection, and regular vulnerability scans.
- **Access Management:** Use role-based access controls (RBAC) and multi-factor authentication (MFA) for AI systems and datasets.

### Ethical Use and Fairness
- **Bias Mitigation:** Use diverse datasets and bias-detection tools during model training. Regularly audit for biases in inputs, algorithms, and outputs.
- **Human Rights:** Ensure AI does not violate human rights, such as freedom of expression or non-discrimination.
- **Prohibited Uses:** AI applications that enable harm, such as surveillance without oversight or manipulative deepfakes, are strictly prohibited.

### Transparency and Explainability
- **Clear Communication:** Use plain language to explain AI capabilities and limitations.
- **Documentation:** Maintain detailed documentation of AI output, including data sources, training methods, and decision logic.
- **User Notifications:** Inform users when AI is involved in decisions affecting them, providing explanations upon request.

### Accountability and Governance
- **Roles and Responsibilities:** Define clear accountability for AI developers, deployers, and users.
- **Reviews:** Establish frameworks for periodic reviews of AI systems, applications, and tools.
- **Reporting Mechanisms:** Incorporate channels for reporting AI-related concerns.

### Safety and Reliability
- **Testing Protocols:** Perform rigorous testing, including edge cases, stress tests, and adversarial robustness checks.
- **Monitoring:** Continuously monitor deployed AI applications and tools for performance degradation or drift.
- **Risk Controls:** Classify AI risks (low, medium, high) and apply appropriate controls.

### Human Oversight and Control
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** Require human review for high-stakes decisions.
- **Override Mechanisms:** Provide easy ways for humans to intervene or override AI outputs.
- **Training for Oversight:** Train personnel on AI limitations and effective oversight.

### Environmental Responsibility
- **Sustainability:** Optimize AI for energy efficiency and use environmentally friendly computing practices.
- **Environmental Reporting:** Track and report AI's carbon footprint where applicable.

### Intellectual Property
- **Ownership:** All AI models, algorithms, outputs, applications, and tools developed by contributors are subject to project licensing terms.
- **Licensing:** Ensure all third-party data, models, or tools are properly licensed.
- **Infringement Protection:** Conduct IP checks before deploying AI systems to avoid infringing on others' rights.

## AI Governance and Lifecycle Management

### Regulatory Compliance
- **Monitoring:** Stay updated on regulations like the EU AI Act, GDPR, and other applicable AI governance frameworks.
- **International Considerations:** Ensure cross-border compliance for global operations.
- **Documentation:** Maintain proper documentation for regulatory inspections and compliance verification.

### Lifecycle Management
- **Governance Checkpoints:** Define and document checkpoints across the AI lifecycle (design, development, deployment, monitoring, retirement).
- **Traceability:** Maintain traceability of decisions and changes throughout the AI lifecycle.
- **Standards Alignment:** Align lifecycle practices with ISO/IEC 42001 and NIST AI RMF standards.

### Third-Party AI Governance
- **Due Diligence:** Vet third-party AI systems for compliance with project standards.
- **Contractual Safeguards:** Include clauses for ethical use, data protection, and accountability.
- **Ongoing Monitoring:** Conduct regular audits and reviews of third-party AI systems.

### Red-Teaming and Adversarial Testing
- **Adversarial Testing:** Simulate misuse scenarios and test against adversarial inputs.
- **Vulnerability Identification:** Identify and document weaknesses in AI systems.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Integrate findings into system updates and security protocols.

### AI Literacy and Culture
- **Education Programs:** Promote AI literacy and responsible practices among contributors.
- **Ethical Awareness:** Foster understanding of AI's societal and legal implications.
- **Open Dialogue:** Encourage feedback and discussion on AI use and governance.

### Public Transparency and Reporting
- **Stakeholder Engagement:** Provide channels for public feedback and concerns.
- **Accountability:** Demonstrate commitment to ethical and responsible AI practices.
- **Transparency Reports:** When possible, publish summary reports of AI use cases, risk assessments, and governance actions.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. They may take corrective actions, including removing or rejecting contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.  

Enforcement actions will be communicated fairly, and repeat or severe violations may result in suspension or bans.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all **community spaces**, including:  
- Git commits, issues, and pull requests  
- Documentation and discussions  
- Project forums and official communication channels  
- AI model development, training, and deployment activities
- Third-party integrations and partnerships
- Public representations of the project

It also applies when representing **Razer AIKit** publicly, such as using official communications or when the project's reputation may be affected.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported to community leaders at [developer@razer.com](mailto:developer@razer.com).  

- All complaints will be handled promptly and fairly  
- Reporters' privacy and security will be respected  
- Cases will be reviewed in line with applicable compliance, legal guidelines, and AI governance frameworks
- Regular audits and reviews will be conducted to ensure adherence to AI principles
- Stakeholder feedback and concerns will be addressed through established channels

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these graduated guidelines:

1. **Correction** – Private written warning, clarification, and education  
2. **Warning** – Formal warning with conditions for continued participation  
3. **Temporary Ban** – Suspension from contributions and discussions for a defined period  
4. **Permanent Ban** – Removal from all community interactions for repeated or severe violations
